The coach behind H'ANN

H'ANN is a coaching brand built on one idea: a strong body comes from a system, not from motivation.

My story

H'ANN started in a small gym with a few clients who wanted more than a workout sheet. They wanted food that worked, training that progressed and someone to answer to. That mix of training, nutrition and a supportive network became Human Advanced Nutrition And Network.

Coaching philosophy

  • Simple plans done consistently beat perfect plans done for a week.
  • Food and training are one program, never two.
  • Every body is different, so every plan is written from scratch.
  • Results are measured, not guessed.

Training + nutrition approach

Training: progressive overload, clean technique and a split matched to your level and time.

Nutrition: protein-first meals from foods you already eat, with calories adjusted every week from your progress.

Network: check-ins and a client community that keeps you accountable.
